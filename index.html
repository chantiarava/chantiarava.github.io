Research profile

I am a Junior Research Fellow at the ICAR–National Bureau of Soil Survey and Land Use Planning (ICAR-NBSS&LUP), Nagpur, working on soil carbon assessment and carbon-credit potential in Indian agriculture. My current research integrates Remote Sensing, GIS, field observations and modelling approaches for mapping and assessing soil carbon.

I hold an M.Sc. in Applied Geography and Geoinformatics from the Central University of Karnataka. Previously, I worked with the Just Transition Research Centre (JTRC), IIT Kanpur, and the Lab for Spatial Informatics (LSI), IIIT Hyderabad.

Research approach

Combining satellite observations, environmental covariates, field measurements and spatial analysis to produce interpretable, map-based research outputs.

What I investigate

Research focus

Research themes across soil systems, environmental change and geospatial decision support.

Soil carbon & digital soil mapping

Spatial prediction of soil organic carbon using field observations, environmental covariates and modelling approaches.

Remote sensing for agriculture

Satellite-derived vegetation and moisture indicators for landscape and agricultural assessment.

Soil erosion & fire impacts

RUSLE-based erosion modelling and assessment of burn severity effects on erosion dynamics.

Land cover & urban climate

Multi-temporal land use/land cover analysis, land surface temperature and urban heat island assessment.

Hydrology & hazards

GIS-based watershed analysis and spatial assessment of flood and landslide vulnerability.

Spatial decision support

Multi-criteria decision analysis, AHP and weighted-overlay methods for suitability mapping.
