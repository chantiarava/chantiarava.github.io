About

I am a Junior Research Fellow at the ICAR – National Bureau of Soil Survey and Land Use Planning (ICAR-NBSS&LUP), Nagpur, working on soil carbon assessment and carbon credit potential in Indian agriculture. My current research focuses on integrating Remote Sensing, GIS, field observations, and modelling approaches for mapping and assessing soil carbon.

I hold an M.Sc. in Applied Geography and Geoinformatics from the Central University of Karnataka. Previously, I worked as a Research Associate at the Just Transition Research Centre (JTRC), IIT Kanpur, and as a Research Assistant and Research Intern at the Lab for Spatial Informatics (LSI), IIIT Hyderabad.

My research interests include soil carbon mapping, digital soil mapping, geospatial modelling, Remote Sensing, GIS, environmental monitoring, land use and land cover change, hydrological applications, climate studies, and the application of geospatial technologies to sustainable agriculture and environmental research.
